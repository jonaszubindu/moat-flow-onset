## B. Per-event eyeball pass (watch `sharp_cea_720s_continuum.mp4`)

Judge for each event, then record keep/drop/trim in the `notes:` field
of `events.yaml` (trim = set t_start/t_end by hand):

1. Pore -> spot transition happens **on disk** (not arriving mature).
2. One trackable leading spot vs ambiguous multi-pore complex.
3. Enough pre-formation baseline inside the |lon| <= 40 deg window.
4. No disqualifiers (immediate decay, merger with another AR, ...).
